## [0.1.0] - 2026-01-28

### Added

- A website for IAS Academy built with React, TypeScript and Vite: courses and course details, pricing, mentorship, optional classes, essays, ethics, value addition, TGPSC and APPSC listings, testimonials, a gallery, and the about, director, contact and privacy policy pages.
- A blog with a listing page and detail pages, in a black colour scheme.
